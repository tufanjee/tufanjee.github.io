About Me

I work as a digital reporter in the Cronkite News Washington, DC bureau, where I report on Congressional proceedings, political developments, and U.S. federal policy from an Arizona perspective.

I am a Nepali journalist with 15 years of experience covering the judiciary, criminal justice, and human rights in both national and international contexts.

Before coming to the United States, I worked as a senior reporter for Kantipur Daily and as a special correspondent for Himal Media, where I reported extensively on the Supreme Court.

My reporting has shaped public debate on justice and governance, and in one case contributed to the release of a man who was wrongfully imprisoned.

I was a 2023 Fulbright Hubert H. Humphrey Fellow at the Walter Cronkite School of Journalism and Mass Communication at Arizona State University, where I completed advanced coursework in data journalism and investigative reporting.

I completed The Lede Program for Data Journalism at Columbia Journalism School in 2025, where I trained in computational analysis, data processing, and statistical methods for reporting.

I also completed a summer journalism program at Universidad Europea de Madrid in Spain in 2025.

In the United States, my professional experience includes data-driven investigations with the Howard Center for Investigative Journalism at the Cronkite School and cross-border reporting through the Borderlands Project in Panama.

My work has been supported by multiple investigative, environmental, and international reporting fellowships, and I have received national journalism awards in Nepal.

My goal is to promote stronger accountability journalism in Nepal using data and public records.
